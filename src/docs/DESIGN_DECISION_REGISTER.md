| D110 | Members-only content (Batch 11 Pass A) | **Members-only section** block (Pro+) hides the blocks below it — everything below, or the next 1–10 — from anyone without access; blocks above stay public. **Members-only page** switch per site page (not home) in the Pages panel, with its own teaser. Access: any live subscriber or chosen plans. Trial, Active, Cancelling (until period end) and Payment failed within grace get in; Paused and Ended don't and are told why (with a link to resume or change plan on their subscriber page). Non-members see the seller's teaser, a Join button (opens sign-up for the one eligible plan, or scrolls to the Subscription block when there are several) and "Already a member? Sign in". **Sign-in without passwords:** phone first (Nigerian numbers in any format normalised to 0803…), code by WhatsApp by default or SMS (for numbers without WhatsApp or on DND), or email; 6-digit code valid 10 min, resend after 30 s, 5 tries; "No membership found" explains what to try. Session lasts 30 days on that device and access is rechecked on every view. Members strip shows plan, "My membership" and Sign out. Draft preview has a Visitor / Member switch. | D112 | Pay on delivery and instalments (Batch 11 Pass C) | Both Pro+, run by the seller (no lender, no cash handling by KUBICU). **Pay on delivery:** delivery orders of physical items only (not pickup, downloads, tickets, DealRooms or made-to-order deposits); seller sets the largest order (default ₦100,000), zones, and cash / transfer / both; phones that refused before can be required to pay online. Order is placed with nothing charged (status New, payment pending, marked "Collect ₦X"), can be packed and booked with a rider; completing it requires recording "collected by cash / transfer" (or the rider's code handover), otherwise "Delivery failed" with a reason returns it to Packing. Transfers carry a warning about fake alerts. Cash never enters the wallet; the KUBICU fee is billed on the plan invoice. **Instalments:** per product 2–4 parts, weekly / every 2 weeks / monthly, send after the first or last part; offered on carts of ₦20,000+ where every item allows it (fewest parts, shortest gap, stricter shipping rule). Equal parts, first absorbs odd naira; explicit consent tick listing dates; card must confirm immediately. Later parts auto-charge while the app is open (outcome follows Simulate next renewal), retries days 1/3/5, then Defaulted. Buyer can pay a part early or update the card (a failed part is taken at once). Seller can retry, record as paid (cash, not wallet) or cancel remaining parts. Unpaid parts aren't counted as paid anywhere; each paid part reaches the wallet as a sale. Wallet refunds now also draw on balances/instalments paid later (closes the D95 gap). Opportunities, notifications and a Reports card. | Proposed | Example figures: ₦100,000 POD cap, ₦20,000 instalment minimum. No interest or fees; instalment wording needs legal review. Card-on-file charges simulated. |
| D113 | Seating maps and repeating events (Batch 11 Pass D) | **Seating (Pro+):** three starting layouts — Theatre rows, Banquet tables (8 × 8, guests can book a whole table, common for weddings, owambe and award dinners), Stage + standing (ticket types with no rows sell by quantity). Buyers choose seats in a Map or a List view (keyboard/screen-reader friendly), up to 10 seats per order and each type's max; exact seats are held 10 min and re-checked at payment so no seat sells twice. Seat shown on ticket, order page, guest list, CSV and check-in. **Repeating (all plans):** weekly / every 2 weeks / monthly, 2–12 dates; each date is its own event (own seats, guest list, check-in, cancel/refund) linked by a series; the Event block shows a date picker of upcoming dates; editing can copy shared details to later dates. Example limits: 30 rows, 40 seats per row. |

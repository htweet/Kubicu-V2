| D50 | Spaces per workspace | A workspace holds one main space plus campaigns and DealRooms. | D51 | Discounts | Three kinds: codes, automatic offers, referral codes. One discount per order; when several apply the customer gets whichever saves more and is told why. | D52 | Campaign pages | A campaign is a one-page space with a window (start optional, end required) and three phases — Before, Live, Ended — derived from the clock, never set by hand. Blocks choose the phases they show in (default: all). The link never breaks: after the end it shows the Ended version until archived (then forwards to the main page). A linked discount code applies automatically at checkout during Live only, and is still re-checked against its own rules. Orders record the link they came from for campaign results. | Proposed | Sign-up counts per campaign and scheduled “it’s live” messages wait for Analytics and real messaging. |
| D53 | Storefront | An auto-built shop at `/shop` on the workspace’s main page (and its other pages), listing every active product with collection tabs, search, sort and an in-stock filter, plus a page per product (`/shop/{id}`) with all photos, options with per-option stock, and one clear buy action. | D54 | DealRooms | One product on its own link, chosen per room as a **limited drop** (a set number of units at a room price, until the deadline or sold out; capped by real stock) or a **group goal** (buyers pay now; orders are held and not fulfilled until the goal is reached; if it’s missed at the deadline every held order is refunded in full automatically and stock returns). Counters only ever use real paid orders — no seeded or padded numbers. Plain terms sit above the buy button; per-person limits match phone numbers. Once anyone has joined, terms lock; the seller may extend the deadline (reason recorded, buyers told) or end early. Updates to buyers are previews. DealRooms need checkout (Pro). Settlement runs in the browser while KUBICU is open; in production it runs on the server at the deadline. | Proposed | Refund fees on a missed goal (D52 caveat) need a payment-provider check.
